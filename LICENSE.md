- **FiQA.** The enterprise scenario shares 57,600 FiQA passages, taken from `BeIR/fiqa`, with every tenant. The FiQA site
  (https://sites.google.com/view/fiqa/) makes its Task 2 data available only for non-commercial use. BEIR's dataset card
  (https://huggingface.co/datasets/BeIR/fiqa) carries a `cc-by-sa-4.0` tag, and BEIR's documentation leaves it to users to
  determine whether they may use a dataset. The prepared corpora are left out of this archive, but a generated answer can
  quote a passage it retrieved, and question lists can hold FiQA queries. Treat those quotations under the source's terms.
- **MS MARCO.** Its terms (https://microsoft.github.io/msmarco/) say the datasets are intended for non-commercial research
  purposes only and are made available without extending any license. The artifacts that hold its text (`x2x5-data-m`,
  with `passages.jsonl.gz` and `queries.json`) are left out of this archive. The MS MARCO result files we opened
  (`rows/*.jsonl.gz`, `pairs.csv`, `config.json`, `subjects.json` listed in the archive of run 40921769714) hold numeric
  identifiers, not text; we sampled them and did not open all 348 archived CI artifacts. If you find MS MARCO text here,
  its terms apply to it.
- **SciDocs.** X6 uses `BeIR/scidocs` for its far chunks, and X3 uses none of it. The dataset card carries a `cc-by-sa-4.0` tag. The prepared corpora are left out
  of this archive, and the X6 records we sampled hold no text field. If you find SciDocs text here, its source's terms apply to it.
- **Answers generated by Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct.** The model is under the Qwen Research License Agreement
