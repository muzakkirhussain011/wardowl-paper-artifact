Permission-aware retrieval draws on literatures that rarely cite one another; each settled part of the design we test, and we found none that tests the whole. Our statements that we found no prior work report
searches, which are not exhaustive. Appendix~\ref{app:related} gives the full account, including native store features and commercial systems such as
Amazon Bedrock's knowledge bases~\cite{aws2026access}, for which we found no independent evaluation of permission correctness.

\paragraph{Enterprise search and databases.}
B\"{u}ttcher and Clarke gave the founding security model for multi-user full-text search: results ranked with global statistics reveal
unreadable files, and query integration fixes it~\cite{buttcher2005security}. Bailey et al.\ modeled late binding and fetched growing
blocks of candidates~\cite{bailey2006secure}. The pre-filter we test is query integration applied to approximate search, and its widening
post-filter is their loop; we claim neither. Query modification~\cite{stonebraker1974access}, Truman semantics~\cite{rizvi2004extending} and
the sound, secure and maximum criteria~\cite{wang2007correctness} frame what a pushed-down filter does and owes. Zerber~\cite{zerr2008zerber}
and searchable encryption~\cite{song2000practical} protect an index from an untrusted server, whereas we trust the store's host. This lineage
gives criteria, not a general proof: Wang et al.\ note that the straightforward extension of authorization-view query modification to queries with negation is not sound, and give a sound and secure algorithm for cell-level policies under exact relational evaluation. We test
translators and approximate indexes, for which we found no such proof.

\paragraph{Authorization.}
In Zanzibar, answering a search query requires ACL checks for all candidate results, Expand lets clients build indexes for access-controlled content, and snapshot tokens guard against applying an old ACL to new content~\cite{pang2019zanzibar}; its descendants added reverse
lookups with documented size limits~\cite{openfga2026search,authzed2026protecting}, and policy engines compile filters through engineering
artifacts~\cite{opa2026evaluating,cerbos2026cerbos}. Margrave~\cite{fisler2005verification}, Zelkova~\cite{backes2018semanticbased} and Cedar's
differential testing~\cite{disselkoen2024how} address the policy and its check path. Cedar's public specification also proves its type-aware partial evaluator sound in Lean~\cite{cedar2026cedarspec}, but for residuals against concrete authorization, not a store's filter language, and the Cedar adapter we test does not use it. We found no peer-reviewed semantics or correctness proof for
any policy-to-filter translator.

\paragraph{Filtered search and permission-aware indexes.}
The collapse of post-filtering is well measured~\cite{patel2024acorn,li2025attribute,kim2026racorn1}. Index designs for attribute-filtered
search~\cite{wang2023efficient,gollapudi2023filtereddiskann,cai2024navigating} take the predicate as part of the query rather than compile it from an
external policy; benchmarks control or measure selectivity and correlation~\cite{zhang2026vecbench,amanbayev2026filtered}, and HCBGen shows that neither alone is a
stable proxy for difficulty~\cite{lim2026revisiting}. None of these uses real ACLs, and neither do we. Permission-specific indexes are recent~\cite{zhong2026honeybee,han2026dont}, and a
vision paper asks for correctness criteria~\cite{yalamarthi2026policyaware}. We propose no index. We found no evaluation of per-query disjunctions over hundreds
or thousands of group labels, and no audit of production stores under such filters at documented, tuned and exact settings.

\paragraph{RAG privacy and agents.}
Anyone who can query a RAG system can drain its store~\cite{zeng2024good,qi2024follow}, and model-side defenses are
probabilistic~\cite{bhatt2025enterprise}. Attribute-based gating at retrieval removed cross-tenant leakage~\cite{arceo2026securing}. Corpus
poisoning~\cite{zou2025poisonedrag} and information-flow control for agents~\cite{costa2025securing} are complementary. We found no test in this literature of whether a layer is faithful or what it costs in recall.

\paragraph{Side channels and testing.}
Our game tests a restricted form of noninterference~\cite{goguen1982security}. Timing has long revealed hidden content on the
web~\cite{felten2000timing,gelernter2015crosssite}, and padding is the simplest case of predictive mitigation~\cite{askarov2010predictive}. On access control over shared indexes the closest work is stronger than ours, on keyword search and relational predicates~\cite{wang2017sidechannel,dar2023rls,rasin2024vulnerability,espiritu2026plaintext}; Section~\ref{sec:side-results} relates our results to it. Database engines
have mature logic-bug oracles~\cite{rigger2020testing,rigger2020detecting}; for vector databases a testing roadmap reports only simplified metamorphic cases~\cite{wang2025towards}, and we found none for filter semantics~\cite{xie2025toward}. Our testing is
differential in McKeeman's sense~\cite{mckeeman1998differential}, and it evaluates its relations under exact search, so that approximation cannot confound them.
