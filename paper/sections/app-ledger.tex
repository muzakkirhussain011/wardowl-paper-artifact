\section{Pre-registration ledger}\label{app:ledger}

Table~\ref{tab:app-ledger} lists every hypothesis and prediction with its registration document, its commit or
amendment, whether it was written before or after the results it concerns, the run that judged it, the verdict as the
analysis script printed it, and a class: \emph{conf.}, confirmatory, written before any result of its experiment;
\emph{expl.}, exploratory, specified in writing after the main results and before running; or \emph{impl.}, an implementation
check of a fix or of the harness. A reader verifies the ordering in two ways: the pre-registration commit is an ancestor of the
run's head commit (\texttt{git merge-base --is-ancestor}), and the GitHub Actions run's creation time follows the commit; Kaggle
runs are ordered by their kernels' version history, and these repositories will be archived. Rules and full wording are in the
pre-registration files. The confirmatory class is relative to a stated earlier exposure: the X1 predictions followed the published
aggregates of X0, the X2 and X5 predictions followed X0, the verdicts of X1's first run and earlier MS MARCO benchmarks in the library's
repository, and the X8 predictions followed X0.
